3 · How you got in

Admission is five acts by the room's owner. They are shown rather than hidden behind a spinner, because the governance is the product.

    –your epoch
    –earliest readable

    Two numbers, because they are different repairs. Behind the room's epoch means a commit has not been delivered. Earliest equal to current means the key chain has not arrived — you can read from where you joined, not before.

    What is real here, and what is not

    Real: your key, the MLS group, the sealing. The broker stores ciphertext and has no way to read it — not a policy, an inability.

    Not yet: the credentials. A real room admits you with credentials the room issues, and a real host authorises every operation against a presentation minted from them. This demo admits anyone who asks.
